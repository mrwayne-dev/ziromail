On your iPhone

Tap the button below on the phone itself. If you are reading this on a computer, scan the code instead.

Install the setup profile
  1. Tap the button. Your phone asks if you want to allow a download — tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings. Near the top you will see Profile Downloaded. Tap it.
  3. Tap Install in the top right, and enter your phone passcode.
  4. Enter your email password when asked. Open the Mail app — your mail is there.

Do not have your password?

Ask whoever set up your account — they can reset it for you in seconds from their dashboard, without contacting support.
