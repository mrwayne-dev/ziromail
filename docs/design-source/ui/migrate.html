How it goes

What actually happens, in order

Day one · 10 minutes

You connect the domain

We generate your records and show you where to paste them. Nothing has changed for your staff yet — mail still arrives where it always did.

Day one · background

We copy your old mail across

Folders, attachments and history move over a secure connection. You watch the progress per mailbox. Your old provider is untouched.

When records pass

The switch happens

Once all four records verify, new mail starts arriving at Ziroemail. Anything still in flight to the old server is collected too.

Same afternoon

Each person gets a link on WhatsApp and taps once. Most teams are fully moved before the end of the working day.

Questions

The things people ask first

Will we lose any email during the move?
No. Your old mailbox keeps receiving until the MX record change takes effect, and anything that arrives there during the changeover is collected afterwards. Nothing is deleted at the old provider either — we copy, we do not move.
Do my staff have to do anything?
Each person sets up their phone once, which takes about thirty seconds from a WhatsApp link. On a computer, Outlook and Apple Mail usually find the new settings themselves after a password prompt.
How long does the whole thing take?
The setup is ten minutes. DNS changes usually take effect within thirty minutes, sometimes a few hours. The mail import runs in the background and depends on how much history you have — a few gigabytes is typically under an hour.
Can we keep our old addresses working for a while?
Yes. It is common to forward the old address into the new mailbox for a month or two while you update letterheads and tell customers. We will set that up with you.
What about calendars and contacts?
Contacts come across with your mail. Calendars work through your phone's own calendar app using the standard protocols — we do not have a built-in calendar screen yet, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.
