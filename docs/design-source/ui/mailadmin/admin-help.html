What to send us

You do not need the technical term for anything. These three things let us trace almost any problem without going back and forth.

  • 1 What happened, in your own words. “Mail to our supplier comes back” is enough to start.
  • 2 A screenshot, if you have one. The part that looks like nonsense is usually the part that tells us the cause, so send it whole.
  • 3 Who it affects and roughly when it started. One person or everybody, since yesterday or since this morning.
