Restoring overwrites

A restore puts the snapshot's contents back in place. Anything received since that snapshot is kept, but anything the customer deleted since then comes back too. Say this out loud to the customer before starting, and take the restore from the most recent snapshot that predates whatever went wrong — not simply the newest one.
