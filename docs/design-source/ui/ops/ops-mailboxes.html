What happens when a mailbox fills up

At 90% the owner is emailed. At 100% incoming mail is held in the queue for 72 hours rather than rejected, so nothing is lost while somebody frees space or raises the quota from the pool. If the pool itself is full, that is a sales conversation, not a support one — 10 GB is ₦1,000 a year.
