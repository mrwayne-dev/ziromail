What trips a flag

Volume more than five times the account's own 30-day average, bounce rate above 5% in an hour, complaints above 0.25%, sending from a country the account has never used, or a successful sign-in after more than 50 failures. Thresholds are per account, not global, so a business that always sends 500 invoices a month is not flagged for doing it again.
