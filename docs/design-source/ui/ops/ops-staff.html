What each role can do

Support

Read any account, answer support messages, re-check DNS records, request a refund, open a customer's admin dashboard read-only.

Cannot lock an account, approve a refund, restore a backup, or read anybody's mail.

Engineer

Everything Support can do, plus lock and unlock accounts, restore backups, and move a domain to the backup relay.

Cannot approve refunds, change staff access, or read anybody's mail.

Owner

Everything, including approving refunds, changing staff access, and — where a customer has asked in writing — opening a mailbox.

Two owners minimum, so nobody can lock themselves out and no single person can act unchecked.

Reading a customer's mail

Only an owner can, only with the customer's written request attached, and the customer is emailed automatically when it happens. The mailbox owner sees a permanent entry in their own activity log. This has been used twice since launch, both times to recover mail after an account compromise, both times at the customer's request.

Both occasions are in the audit trail
