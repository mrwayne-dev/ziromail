Why the reason field is compulsory

An entry saying “account locked” is almost useless six months later. An entry saying why, by whom, and what the customer was told is a defence if the decision is ever questioned — by the customer, by a regulator, or by us reviewing our own judgement. Entries are kept for seven years.
