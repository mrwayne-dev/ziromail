What happens next

Three steps and your business is running on Ziroemail

  • 1
    Confirm your email One click on the link we send you.
  • 2
    Connect your domain We generate the exact records to paste, show you where they go in your registrar, and check them for you. Or hand it over and we will do it.
  • 3
    Add your staff Create their mailboxes and send each person a setup link on WhatsApp. Their phone configures itself.

Your mailboxes do not go live until your domain records check out. That is deliberate — it is the difference between mail that reaches the inbox and mail that lands in spam.
