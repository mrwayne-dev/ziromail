Questions

Before you decide

Can two of my domains share one plan?
No. Each domain needs its own plan. Storage is shared freely between mailboxes on the same domain, but never across domains — that separation is what stops one domain's sending problems affecting another's delivery.
How many mailboxes can I create?
As many as your pool supports. Ten staff on the 10 GB Starter plan works if their quotas add up to 10 GB. Give one person the whole pool if that suits you better — we do not charge per seat.
What happens when the pool runs out?
We warn you at 80% and again at 95%. Add the storage you need and it is available immediately, or lower a mailbox quota to free space. Mail is not deleted because a pool filled up — new mail queues while you sort it out.
Can I change how storage is split later?
Yes, whenever you like, from your dashboard. Lowering a quota below what a mailbox already uses is blocked until that mailbox clears some space, so nothing is lost.
What if I reach my plan's ceiling?
Starter tops out at 25 GB and Business at 100 GB. At that point you move to the next plan, keeping the same domain and mailboxes — only the pool changes. Premium has no ceiling.
Do you have a free trial?
Not at the moment, and we would rather explain why than pretend it is an oversight. Free mailboxes are how spammers get into a mail service, and one of them can get every other customer's mail blocked. Every account here is paid and tied to a domain you can prove you own.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us how many staff you have and roughly how much mail you keep. We will tell you which plan fits — including if that is the cheapest one.
